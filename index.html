Master's thesis

Supersingular Reductions of Elliptic Curves over Global Fields

M2 thesis, Sorbonne University, 2025 · Supervisor: Gregorio Baldi · Mention Très Bien

Read the thesis (PDF, 73 pages)

A study of geometrically isogenous reductions of elliptic curves over number fields, following François Charles's theorem that any two elliptic curves over a number field have geometrically isogenous reductions at infinitely many finite places, and of a supersingularity result that follows. The thesis also treats related supersingularity results for odd-degree number fields and for function fields, using Arakelov geometry, Hecke correspondences, equidistribution, local intersection theory, Serre–Tate theory and deformation theory.

Research interests

Arithmetic geometry and number theory, in particular elliptic curves and abelian varieties, their reductions, modular curves, and Arakelov theory.
